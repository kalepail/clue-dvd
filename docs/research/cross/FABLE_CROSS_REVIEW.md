## 1. The evidence capsule — ACCEPT the mechanism, REJECT two of its proposed enforcement regimes

All four reports endorse a deterministic write-down capsule beside Ashe's narration (DVD-authentic per the brief). I agree it is the correct truth boundary. Two consensus elaborations should not survive:

1. **REJECT fail-closed narration suppression (SOL Phase 1: "suppress/fallback unsafe narration rather than accepting residual errors").** Once the capsule is the canonical record, prose drift is *deductively harmless by construction* — suppression buys no additional truth and reintroduces the exact failure V3 was built to escape: players staring at machine sentences ("template prose felt mechanical", `AI_ENGINE_V3_PLAN.md:13`). Fail-closed only for the existing hard check (unlicensed card names, already repaired per-clue at `ai-mystery-engine.ts:311-365`). Everything softer is telemetry.
2. **REJECT heuristic semantic gates (Terra §11.2's polarity/negation vocabulary check).** The canonical `room_undisturbed` brief is *built from negations* — "nothing out of place, nothing missing, nothing so much as nudged" (`fact-harvest.ts:509`). A negation-proximity heuristic false-positives on the most common fact kind in the game and would trigger constant repairs/suppressions → flattened prose via the side door. The capsule makes these heuristics unnecessary; don't build both.

Also flag a **fake-rigor acceptance criterion**: SOL F2's "zero unsupported claims in the capsule across 100 adversarial mocked renders" is vacuously true — the capsule is generated from the `Fact`, not the render; no mock can put a claim into it. The real acceptance is UI-level: capsule displayed for 100% of reveals, persisted in the log, and players in blind tests cite the capsule (not the prose) when resolving disputes. Precede all of this with a ~30-seed `--ai` hand audit of actual scope/polarity drift (`scripts/eval-mysteries.ts` `--ai` mode) — the panel prescribes heavy machinery for a failure rate nobody has measured.

## 2. Closing honesty — ACCEPT, but adopt SOL's framing, not the public-only-recap purism

Consensus fix: feed the closing only Butler briefs + a public-only field. Two corrections:

- **SOL is the only report that caught the early-solve path** (`useGameStore.ts:641-685` ends the game on a correct accusation at any reveal count), which breaks even a Butler-only "revealed during play" recap. The fix that survives: phrase the closing as **post-solve reconstruction from the case file** ("the record shows…"), never as witnessed history — then one pre-generated closing is honest under all reveal counts and note-access states. ACCEPT SOL F13's framing; REJECT any runtime path-specific closing generation (network call at the moment of victory; failure mode at the worst possible time).
- **Guard the payoff.** The public-only field is broad (7.83 suspects / 7.75 locations mean — reproduced independently by three reports). A closing that narrates only that breadth reads as "the app never solved anything." The reconstruction should lean on the two-to-three strongest *public* chains plus explicit credit to suggestions and dealt cards — the physical-card finish is the climax, not an apology. Acceptance: blind-table survey item "the ending felt earned" does not regress vs. baseline.

## 3. Best-of-K diversity — ACCEPT the mechanism, flag the panel's Goodhart trap and one missing objective

All four reports converge on best-of-K/lexicographic selection over already-valid schedules. Correct and cheap. Three challenges:

1. **The numeric targets are manufactured confidence.** Terra: "no kind >25%"; CODEX: "≤30%", "converged pair ≤70%"; SOL: "≤35%", "<80%", "adjacent-axis repeats −50%". None is derived from measured candidate variance. The harvest pool is structurally ~50% accounting facts (`fact-harvest.ts:331-592`), and location convergence has almost no supply (one optional closure per world; location+time converged in only 21/500 cases). Selection can only choose among what harvest offers — several of these targets are **likely infeasible before Phase-3 supply lands, and the roadmap gates would fail through no fault of the selector**. Required first step: a variance probe — for 100 seeds generate K=8 valid schedules and measure the *spread* of kind-entropy/note-identity/converged-pair across candidates. Set targets from the probe's 75th percentile, staged pre-supply vs. post-supply.
2. **Within-case diversity is not between-case variety — the selector can create a new monoculture one level up.** If every case maximizes the same kind-entropy scalar, every case converges to the same "pleasantly mixed" shape: ~30% items, one thread, one closure, item+time finish. That is uniformity wearing a diversity costume. This is precisely the Quality-Diversity lesson in `EXTERNAL_DESIGN_EVIDENCE.md:35-52`: describe a behavior space and cover it, don't optimize one scalar. Concrete, cheap implementation: compute a **schedule-shape signature** (converged pair + top-2 kinds + thread-staging + note kinds) and add *distance from the last N cases' signatures* as a selector objective — the `recentSignatures` plumbing already exists for prose (`ai-mystery-engine.ts:150-156`) and should carry the schedule shape too. No panel report states this crisply; without it, Phase-1 metrics will improve while replay sameness persists.
3. **Kind labels ≠ perceived variety.** `item_home` vs `item_intact` are different enum values and nearly identical table sentences. Entropy over `FactKind` is a proxy that can be gamed by relabeling. Keep it, but the binding metric is the blind-table "could you retell three clues an hour later / did cases feel alike" survey, and mechanism-family grouping (SOL's `mechanismFamily`) should collapse near-synonyms *before* entropy is computed.

## 4. Occasion truth-conditioning — MODIFY: overlays yes, WorldArchetype layer not yet

Consensus that occasion must alter deterministic truth is sound (50/50 byte-identical worlds, SOL's probe). Two dissents:

- **SOL's `WorldArchetype` (beatGrammar, roomRoles, itemPolicies, threadTemplates, sourceTargets, physicalEventPolicy) is a second world-engine.** Six archetypes × seven config surfaces is the CaseBible failure shape rebuilt in TypeScript — deterministic this time, but the same scope creep the plan itself warns against (`AI_ENGINE_V3_PLAN.md:183`). Terra/CODEX's catalog overlays (family-keyed gathering tables, activity pools, thread causes, display bias) deliver the measurable topology change at a tenth of the surface. Adopt overlays; graduate to archetypes only if overlays fail their divergence gate.
- **Table-perceptibility is unproven.** A recital biasing gatherings toward the Ballroom changes one noun in two clues. Per MDA (`EXTERNAL_DESIGN_EVIDENCE.md:27-33`), this is authored-content change whose dynamics-layer effect is hypothesis, not fact. Add to the acceptance gate: in blind tests, players describe the case's occasion unprompted post-game (≥50% of tables). If overlays pass topology-divergence but fail this, stop investing in world texture and reweight toward the event economy.
- **REJECT SOL F4's classifier gate** ("structural leakage classifier must predict answer no better than chance+5pp"). A weak classifier passes the gate and certifies nothing — Goodhart by construction. Replace with the concrete mechanical fix (allow non-thief occupancy of the answer room at non-theft slots; the per-slot guard at `world-sim.ts:517-519` already exists, the all-day exclusion at `:410` is redundant) plus direct proxies: mean answer-room positive mentions per case, and the existing suspect-mention spread penalty (`clue-scheduler.ts:740-756`).

## 5. Threads — ACCEPT staging, adjudicate the panel's safety disagreement

Conflict: Terra requires all thread stages stay mention-only (zero kill weight, locked by test); my primary report floated constraining payoffs (alibi-type); SOL wants the answer suspect eligible for benign threads (current code excludes them, `world-sim.ts:641`) and payoffs that "do not claim innocence beyond the modeled incident."

Ruling: **Terra's conservative rule wins for the first release** — all stages mention-only, payoff mandatory before position 12, one featured thread per case. A constraining payoff interacting with an answer-suspect-inclusive thread is the single easiest place in this whole program to manufacture an accidental alibi for the thief; keep those two upgrades apart and property-test each alone. SOL's answer-suspect-eligibility is directionally right (blanket exclusion is a meta-signature) but is imperceptible within one case at ≤2 thread facts scheduled — do it in Phase 4 with the guard that any thread containing the answer suspect carries no timeId/locationId equal to the answer's. ACCEPT SOL's observation as real, LOW priority.

## 6. Notes — ACCEPT jobs + non-vacuousness; REJECT the four-view validation apparatus

SOL's finding that 32/1,000 notes kill zero live cells is the best note-level catch on the panel — a private note that changes nothing is a wasted turn purchase; require every note to reduce live cells (cheap selector filter). Note-kind distinctness (N1.kind ≠ N2.kind): ACCEPT, 4/4 consensus.

But **SOL's KnowledgeView validation of all four partitions (public/+N1/+N2/both) as hard gates is over-engineering**: checkpoints are lower bounds on survivors and facts only ever kill, so any subset of reveals trivially satisfies them — there is no fairness failure mode a partial view can create. The only partition property worth machinery is the **public-only convergence floor** (upper-bound windows on the Butler-only end state so a note-averse table still converges), feasibility-swept before enforcement. One scheduler check, not a framework.

## 7. Turn shell — ACCEPT restoration; REJECT per-turn move confirmation; flag the friction falsifier

4/4 endorse authentic action identity (rename Reveal Clue → Summon the Butler, pantry-draw ritual, Evidence-Room bar, item-card penalty on both UIs with acknowledgement and host-declared elimination, passages as movement with the roll as labeled variant, post-turn event timing, `roomClosure`→`lockedRooms` wiring). ACCEPT all.

**REJECT SOL's MoveConfirm phase.** The app tracks no pawn position, so a per-turn movement acknowledgement validates nothing and is pure ritual theater — one extra tap × every turn × every player, added to a shell whose whole appeal is lightness. The board enforces movement; the app should enforce only what it can see (action identity and its rituals). Keep SOL's own excellent falsifier and apply it to everything in this phase: median added turn overhead ≤20s, missed acknowledgements <5% — I predict MoveConfirm fails it and the five-action menu passes.

Terra's note-gating unification (GamePage `>=5/7` vs store `0.5/0.65`, latent divergence if clue count ≠ 10): ACCEPT, trivial.

## 8. The pantry arithmetic couples cadence and the event economy — a hidden dependency no report priced

10 item cards sit in the Butler's Pantry (11 minus the solution card). Ten scheduled Butler clues delivered via summons consume exactly 10 draws — **the current cadence spends the entire pantry**. The rulebook's event placements *also* draw from the pantry ("DVD events move facedown item cards from the Pantry to board locations", brief:188), and `TOPROMPT.md:13` remembers the original allowing only ~7 summons. So the consensus Phase-5 event economy and the current 10-summon cadence are **arithmetically incompatible**: you cannot place 2–3 cards on the board *and* fund 10 summon draws from a 10-card pantry. Corollary: the corpus's universal 8-testimony count is plausibly not a stylistic choice but the *residue of the pantry after event placements* — which reframes F-S5/cadence from "optional aesthetic experiment" (Terra §1.9) to "forced decision inside Phase 5." SOL's Phase 5 gates on pantry-path validation and pairs the cadence pilot with events — ACCEPT that pairing and make it explicit: before any event placement ships, choose one delivery model: (a) 8-summon cadence with 2 placements (corpus-shaped), (b) 10 clues where post-placement summons deliver testimony without a draw (house-labeled), or (c) events that relocate rather than add net draws. Acceptance: the conservation equation SOL wrote (pantry + board + hands + Evidence Room = 10) holds on every reachable path, *and* every scheduled clue has a reachable delivery trigger.

## 9. Priority disagreement adjudicated: converged-axis monoculture

Terra grades item+time monoculture "minor, closer to feature than bug"; Fable/SOL/CODEX grade it important. Terra's own principles decide this against Terra: 57.8% of games end with times fully solved by public evidence, making every dealt *time* card systematically worthless — a direct erosion of the physical-card finish Terra elsewhere treats as sacrosanct, and a meta-telegraph (the anchor-bundle → theft-hour pattern) for repeat players. Keep it important — but staged honestly: it is **supply-limited** (needs location-cutting and time-flexible facts from Phase 3), so it cannot be a Phase-1 gate. `AI_ENGINE.md:37`'s "which two varies by seed" claim should be corrected now regardless.

## 11. The revised plan that survives these objections

**P0 — Measure before machinery (days).** Doc fixes (scheduler header, plan adjacency line, `AI_ENGINE.md:37` axis-variety claim, GAME_REFERENCE turn rules; quarantine V2 docs). `verifyClosing` boundary fix. Closing → reconstruction framing with Butler-only recap + public-only field (SOL F13 shape). Delete-or-wire `NON_CARD_NAME_WHITELIST`. Extend eval telemetry (kind/note/pair/thread/negative-space) and freeze baseline. **Run the two probes that gate later phases:** 30-seed AI drift audit (does prose actually invert scope/polarity, and how often?) and best-of-K variance probe (how much diversity exists among valid schedules today?). Acceptance: probes documented; all targets in P2/P4 rewritten from probe percentiles.

**P1 — Shell fidelity, light (1–2 sessions).** Five authentic actions with rituals; summon rename + pantry-draw prompt + Evidence-Room bar; accusation completion (item-card wording, phone parity, acknowledgement log, host-declared elimination); passages as movement (roll → labeled variant, seeded); note re-read parity + gating unification; `roomClosure`→`lockedRooms`; structured 3-of-4 suggestion log; post-turn-only interruptions. **No MoveConfirm.** Acceptance: SOL's friction gate (≤20s overhead, <5% missed acks) + state-machine tests.

**P2 — Truth channel + selector (1–2 sessions).** Evidence capsule with progressive disclosure (narration performs, capsule persists in log; no heuristic suppression). Best-of-K selector with probe-derived targets, note non-vacuousness, note-kind distinctness, public-only convergence floor (feasibility-swept), and **schedule-shape signature distance vs. recent cases** (the between-case objective). Acceptance: probe-derived metric movement + blind-table dispute-resolution via capsule + "cases feel alike" survey improvement.

**P3 — Supply (2–3 sessions).** Occasion catalog overlays (not archetypes); item lifecycle reskins + `item_moved`/`key_event` kinds; answer-room all-day exclusion relaxed to answer-time-only; thread stage-facts (mention-only, fused briefs split at harvest). Property tests per new kind; golden-corpus #3/#10 encodings. Acceptance: per-family topology divergence AND unprompted occasion recognition at table; accounting share target from P0 probe.

**P4 — Shaping (1–2 sessions).** Staged-thread scheduling (setup ≤4, payoff ≥8, one arc/case); converged-pair steering (now supply-backed); note jobs (lens/keystone); answer-suspect thread eligibility with theft-hour guard. Acceptance: ≥60% of cases carry a complete arc; converged-pair distribution vs. steering weights; meta-telegraph probe drops toward chance.

**P5 — Event economy + cadence, decided together (2–3 sessions).** Post-turn event queue (locks/unlocks, facedown placements, Look at Item Card, Inspector's Challenge), truth-neutral by construction (events move cards/access, never assert facts). **Entry gate: the pantry delivery model (§8) is chosen and conservation-tested before any placement ships.** The 8-testimony cadence pilot runs inside this phase, not as a separate aesthetic experiment.

Standing constraints across all phases: deterministic truth and the answer-blind renderer untouched; no answer-aware prose beyond the closing; physical cards must settle at least one axis in every case; every new hard constraint feasibility-swept on 500 seeds before enforcement; every phase must not regress schedule success ≥99.8% or any existing invariant test.

## 12. Bottom line

The panel converged on the right architecture verdict and the right defect list; its residual risks are *calibration* risks: unmeasured targets (three different made-up entropy ceilings), safety machinery that would flatten the prose it protects (fail-closed narration, negation heuristics), diversity scalars that manufacture a second-order monoculture (no between-case objective), one unpriced arithmetic coupling (pantry vs. cadence vs. events), and two vacuous acceptance criteria (capsule mutation tests, leakage classifier). The revised plan keeps every unanimous fix, reorders nothing structural, and replaces each of those five with a probe, a scoped-down mechanism, or an explicit decision gate.
